HOW TO PLAY

Hand Cricket Rules

1
Win the Toss

Choose Heads or Tails to decide who gets the first choice.

2
Choose a Number

Select a number from 1 to 6 when batting or bowling.

3
Score Runs

If the numbers are different, the batter scores the batting number.

4
Get Out

If both numbers are the same, the batter is out and the innings ends.

5
Win the Match

After both innings, the player with the higher score wins.
